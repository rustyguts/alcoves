## Banff, April

We landed in Calgary and drove out the same afternoon. Lake Louise was still
half frozen — the **turquoise water** only shows at the edges this early.

- [x] Book the Moraine Lake shuttle
- [x] Sunrise at Two Jack Lake
- [ ] Come back in September for the larches

Best coffee: *Little Brick* on the main strip. Get there before 9.

## Tokyo, June

Three days was not enough. Notes for next time:

1. Stay in Yanaka — quieter, and the morning market is worth it
2. The Shibuya crossing photo works best from the station walkway
3. Day trip to Nikko instead of a fourth museum day

> The city rewards walking without a plan more than any itinerary.
